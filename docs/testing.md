# Test your app

Test your app before releasing it in the app stores. A mix of testing approaches can help your team catch problems early and make sure the app works well for everyone:

- **Accessibility testing** checks that people can use the app, including people who use assistive technologies.
- **Beta testing** lets a select group of people try the app and share feedback before its public release.
- **End-to-end testing** checks that common tasks work from beginning to end
- **Security testing** looks for weaknesses that could put the app or users' information at risk.
- **Unit testing** checks that individual parts of the app work as intended.

Apple and Google both provide tools for beta testing. For details, see [Testing your mobile application](developer.md#testing-your-mobile-application) section in the [Mobile app development guide](developer.md).
